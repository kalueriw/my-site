Five things I might build

One idea is not a choice — it is a decision already made for you. Five is enough to compare: each one does a specific job, and says what would make it worth building.

5 ideas · Plain HTML and CSS

The ideas

Order does not matter

  • Campus map

    A map where you can search a building by the room number on your timetable and see the shortest door-to-door route, indoors.

  • Ingredient-fusion recipes

    Type in the three things left in your fridge; get a recipe that actually uses them, with a substitution for whatever you do not have.

  • Browser mini-game

    A five-minute game that loads in one page and plays with the arrow keys — no download, no account, no tutorial.

  • Campus forum

    One place to ask about a course, a room or a club — and get an answer from a student who was there last year.

  • Pet chooser

    A guide to choosing a pet that fits your life: answer six questions about your room, your hours and your budget, and get three animals that would suit it — plus one that would not.
