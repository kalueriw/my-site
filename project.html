Five things I might build

One idea is not a choice. Five is enough to compare — each does a specific job and says why it is worth building.

5 ideas · Plain HTML and CSS

The ideas

Order does not matter

  • Campus map

    Search a building by the room number on your timetable and get the shortest indoor route.

  • Ingredient-fusion recipes

    Type in three things left in your fridge; get a recipe that uses them, with substitutions.

  • Browser mini-game

    A five-minute game in one page, played with the arrow keys — no download, account or tutorial.

  • Campus forum

    Ask about a course, a room or a club; get an answer from a student who was there.

  • Pet chooser

    Answer six questions about your room, hours and budget; get three pets that fit — and one that does not.
